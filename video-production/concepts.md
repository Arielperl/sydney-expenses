## v2 — "מרעש לבהירות" (selected)

- **Premise:** Every sale is a beat; too many beats become noise; one mint sweep turns the noise into the product.
- **Opening hook:** "₪180" slams onto a black screen on frame 1 with a tuned terminal beep; three more amounts follow on the beat.
- **Structure:** sales (0–4) → noise and questions (4–7.75) → silence → drop and sweep (8) → four product beats (10–25) → the full picture and CTA (25–30).
- **Visual identity:** dark deep-green 3-D space; mint appears only after the drop.
- **Tone:** energetic, confident, clean.
- **Text:** big kinetic Hebrew headlines, all taken from the website's own copy.
- **Music / sound:** 120 BPM melodic electronic; beeps → riser → silence → drop; sonic logo B4–E5–G♯5.
- **Main advantage:** punchy, product-forward, 100% accurate, fully rendered with no placeholders.
- **Challenge:** it has no human face, so the emotion comes from rhythm and release rather than a character.
- **Duration:** 30 s (+15 s, 6 s). **Assets:** only existing project assets.

Full direction: `selected-concept.md`.

## Concept A — "אותו ערב, פעמיים" (Same Evening, Twice) ★ Recommended

**Premise:** We watch a studio owner's evening at 23:40 twice — once chasing the day's sales, once when they have already found their place — and the only thing that changed is Sydney.

**Opening hook (0–3 s):** Three macro shots of payments at the studio counter — card, phone, card. Each beep is a musical note (B4, E5, G♯5); by the third beep the melody is recognizable and the music begins.

**Narrative structure:** Day (sales happen, she's busy teaching) → Night 1, 23:40 (the chase) → black, silence, "אותו יום." + brand → the same payment, now arriving in the product by itself; the figure updates → Night 2, 23:40, same frame (calm, handled) → lamp off → end card.

**Visual identity:** One real-feeling location (a Tel Aviv pilates studio). Practical light. Night 1 is cold-vs-warm and handheld; Night 2 is harmonized and locked-off. Product UI appears as full-frame inserts on the brand's deep green.

**Emotional tone:** Quiet, recognizable, gently relieving. No drama.

**On-screen text:** 8 short elements (2–4 words each), mostly questions and product-site phrases; the time tag "23:40" is repeated to anchor the device.

**Music:** Felt piano + pad + mallet motif derived from the terminal beeps; drone in Night 1, silence at the turn, pulse in the product section, resolution in Night 2.

**Sound design:** Intimate foley (pencil, thermal paper, lamp switch, lid close) — the before is noisy with small anxieties, the after is almost silent.

**Main advantage:** The before/after is *structural* (same time, same frame), so it reads instantly without sound or text. The human hero carries emotion; the product carries proof.

**Production challenge:** Keeping the same woman, wardrobe and room consistent across ~11 AI-generated shots.

**Estimated duration:** 45 s (plus 15 s and 6 s cutdowns).

**Asset requirements:** Character + location reference plates, 11 AI video shots (one reused), 4 motion-graphics UI inserts, end card, logo (existing), score + foley.

## Concept B — "החוט" (The Thread)

**Premise:** Every sale the business makes leaves a thin mint thread of light that travels through the city at dusk and weaves itself into one clear line — the owner's picture of the day.

**Opening hook:** A card taps a terminal; a hair-thin mint line snaps out of the terminal and races across the counter, out the window, into the street.

**Narrative structure:** Threads multiply (sales across the day) → at night the threads on her desk are knotted in papers and screens → she connects one thing → threads straighten and braid into the product's trend line → the line becomes the logo's arrow → CTA.

**Visual identity:** Live action + a single VFX element (the thread), directly echoing the website hero image's mint wave.

**Emotional tone:** Poetic, magical-realist.

**On-screen text:** Minimal — 3–4 lines.

**Music:** Continuous rising string-and-synth ostinato that "tightens" as threads align.

**Sound design:** A tonal "string" sound for each thread; knotted threads = detuned; aligned = in tune.

**Main advantage:** Highly ownable visual metaphor that connects to the existing hero artwork.

**Production challenge:** Needs real VFX compositing; AI-generated light threads tend to look like cheap "tech" animation — exactly what the brand must avoid. Metaphor may read as "growth/finance magic" rather than a concrete product.

**Estimated duration:** 40 s.

**Asset requirements:** Live action plates, VFX thread simulation (Houdini/AE Trapcode), tracking, compositing.

## Concept C — "שאלות של 23:40" (The 23:40 Questions)

**Premise:** A typographic film built from the questions an owner asks at night — each question answered, calmly, by the product itself.

**Opening hook:** A blinking cursor in the dark; the question "כמה באמת נכנס היום?" types itself, then more questions stack and overlap faster.

**Narrative structure:** Questions pile up (tension) → a single mint pulse clears the screen → the product answers one question at a time with real UI (sales list, net receipts breakdown, attention list, assistant) → final question "ומה עכשיו?" → CTA.

**Visual identity:** Pure motion design in the brand system: IBM Plex Sans Hebrew, deep green, mint accents, real UI.

**Emotional tone:** Smart, crisp, reassuring.

**On-screen text:** Text-led by design (the questions *are* the visuals) — heavier reading load.

**Music:** Minimal electronic pulse, typing-rhythm percussion.

**Sound design:** Soft key clicks, UI ticks, a resolving chord per answer.

**Main advantage:** Fully producible in-house, highly accurate, inexpensive, perfectly on-brand; easy to localize and update.

**Production challenge:** No human hero — weaker emotional pull; risks feeling like an animated product tour.

**Estimated duration:** 30 s.

**Asset requirements:** Product UI capture, motion design, score.

## Recommendation — Concept A, "אותו ערב, פעמיים"

**Why it's the most effective.** Its core device — identical time, identical frame, different evening — makes the transformation unmistakable in a single viewing and with the sound off. It follows the full arc (hook → tension → recognition → discovery → transformation → confidence → action) with one idea only: *you don't have to chase your sales.*

**Why it fits the brand.** Sydney's voice is calm and honest (the website's former eyebrow line). Concept A has no magic, no inflated numbers and no hype — just an evening that got shorter. Its closing line is the website's own hero line, so the film and the site speak with one voice.

**Why it fits the audience.** Israeli small-business owners know the 23:40 counter personally. The owner is the hero; the product is the quiet guide that appears only when needed.

**Why it can be produced realistically.** One location, one principal character, one wardrobe (same day), mostly static or slow camera work — the conditions under which current AI video models (Veo 3 / Kling / Runway Gen-4 with reference images) are most consistent. The master wide is one composition reused three times. All UI is built as motion graphics (never AI-generated). The 45 s animatic, typography, end card and temp score are already rendered in this package.

**What could make it fail.**
1. The AI-generated woman drifts between shots (face, hair, cardigan).
2. The Night 1 section feels like a stock "stressed person" cliché.
3. The product section feels like a slideshow of screenshots.
4. Viewers don't grasp that Night 2 is "the same evening, with Sydney."

**How each risk is prevented.**
1. Generate a locked character reference sheet and location plates first; use image-to-video with those references for every shot; keep faces at CU in only two shots (S07/S15), which are the same angle so they can be generated in one session from the same start frame; hide faces in wides by distance/backlight.
2. Keep Night 1 behavior small and specific (a crossed-out sum, two fingers to the brow, an exhale) — no head-in-hands, no dramatic sighs, no looking at camera.
3. Tie UI to cause and effect: the *same* card tap from the opening triggers the new sale row, and that row visibly updates the net figure. Motion uses the product's own easing, not flashy transitions.
4. Repeat the identical "23:40" tag and identical wide composition; announce the turn with silence + "אותו יום." + the brand lockup.
