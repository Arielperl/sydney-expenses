## 7. Camera

- Sensor look: large-format digital cinema (ARRI Alexa 35-like), 24 fps, 180° shutter (natural motion blur).
- Lenses: spherical primes with gentle, slightly vintage softness (Cooke S4-like). 35 mm for wides, 50 mm for mediums/over-the-shoulder, 85 mm for face close-ups, 100 mm macro for inserts.
- Depth of field: T2–T2.8. Inserts have a razor-thin focal plane.
- Movement: Day = slow slider/dolly (≤ 5 cm/s). Night 1 = handheld breathing (±1–2% frame drift, no shake). Night 2 = locked-off tripod. Never drones, zooms, whip pans or speed ramps.
- Horizon: level everywhere except Night 1 close shots (≤ 2° off-level).

## 8. Image texture

Fine organic 35 mm-style grain (lighter by day, slightly stronger at night). Soft halation around practicals (lamp, terminal glow, streetlight). No lens flares beyond a faint veil from the lamp. No sharpening halos. Skin shows real texture (pores, fine lines) — never airbrushed.

## 9. Motion behaviour (people and things)

Real-time motion only. No slow motion except S01 (card tap) at 48 fps conformed to 24 fps (0.5× speed) for 1.75 s — motivated by the macro. Hands move with purpose and pause naturally. Steam rises slowly. Paper curls stay still unless touched.

## 10. Screen direction

"Forward/progress" = **right → left**. Clients leave toward frame-left (the door). The owner exits to frame-left in S16. New UI rows slide in from the right. Text is right-aligned.

## 11. Typography system

| Style | Font | Weight | Size @1080p (16:9) | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | IBM Plex Sans Hebrew | SemiBold 600 | 64 px, line-height 1.2, tracking 0 | `#FFFFFF` (dark frames) / ink `#151A19` (bright day frames — T1 only) | All main text elements |
| Emphasis | same | SemiBold 600 | as headline | Mint `#41D7B2` | Only "בתמונה" on the end card |
| Supporting | IBM Plex Sans Hebrew | Regular 400 | 30 px | `#FFFFFF` at 72% | T5 sub-line |
| Time tag | IBM Plex Sans (Latin figures) | Medium 500, tabular | 30 px, tracking +4% | Mint `#41D7B2` | "23:40" |
| Brand lockup | IBM Plex Sans Hebrew / Plex Sans | 600 / 500 small caps "SYDNEY" tracking 16% | 40 / 16 px | White / 60% white | T4, end card |
| URL | IBM Plex Sans | Medium 500 | 26 px | White 80% | End card, isolated LTR |

**Animation (one system):** Enter = opacity 0→1 + translateY 12 px→0 over 400 ms, ease-out-quart `cubic-bezier(0.25,1,0.5,1)`. Exit = opacity 1→0 over 300 ms, ease-in. Two-line elements stagger 120 ms. No typewriter effects, no scaling, no blur-ins, no glows.

**Contrast treatment:** white text sits only on dark, clean areas and over night footage gets a soft radial scrim (black at 35%, 600 px radius) — never a box. Over the bright day wall (T1), use ink `#151A19` with no scrim.

**Safe margins:** 16:9 title-safe = 10% (192 px horizontal / 108 px vertical). 9:16: keep 14% top, 35% bottom, 6% sides free of text.
