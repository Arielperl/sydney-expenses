# Sound Design & Music (v2)

No narration, dialogue, vocals or lyrics. The temp track `assets/music/temp-score-*.wav` is synthesized from scratch by `tools/score.py`; it contains no samples or third-party material. **The final film should use a licensed or commissioned track built on the same cue points.**

## Concept

**The sound of a sale is the hook, the noise is the problem, and the drop is the product.** Terminal beeps tuned to E major start the film as a melody (B4 · E5 · G♯5 · B5). They multiply into noise, stop dead, and the product arrives on a drop. The same three notes return as the sonic logo.

- **Genre:** modern melodic electronic / tech-house-lite (clean kick, clap, crisp hats, side-chained pad and bass, a pluck motif). Confident, not aggressive, and never "epic trailer".
- **Tempo / key:** 120 BPM, E major (bar = 2.00 s, so 30 s = 15 bars).

## Mixing priorities and loudness

1. Beeps / sonic logo → 2. kick + bass → 3. UI sounds → 4. pad/pluck → 5. whooshes.
- Pad and bass are side-chained to the kick (−5 to −10 dB, 90–110 ms release).
- Delivery: **−14 LUFS integrated, ≤ −1 dBTP** (temp mixes measured at −14.0 LUFS, TP −1.3 to −1.4).
- Cutdowns are spliced from the same mix at the same source times as the picture, so sync is exact.

## Licensing

- **Recommended:** commission a producer for a 30 s cue, stems, 15 s and 6 s edits and a standalone sonic logo (B4–E5–G♯5 → E), as a buy-out covering paid social worldwide.
- **Library alternative:** Musicbed / Artlist / Epidemic / Soundstripe with a **paid-advertising** license; conform the cut points (4.00, 7.75 gap, 8.00 drop, 25.00 breakdown) to the track's bars and re-tune the beeps to its key.
- Never use vocals, chops of speech, choirs, or unlicensed trending audio.
