# Creative Brief — "אותו ערב, פעמיים" (Same Evening, Twice)

Client: מנהל הכנסות מבית Sydney · Market: Israel · Language: Hebrew (RTL) · No voice, no dialogue, no lyrics.
Decisions confirmed by the client (2026-09-24): hybrid production (AI video live action + real product UI as motion graphics) · website + Meta ads · CTA → `sydney-revenue-manager.vercel.app` · hero = small studio owner.

---

## The single most important message

**The business sells all day; you don't have to chase the numbers at night — the picture is already there.**
In the brand's own words (website hero, `HomePage.tsx`): **העסק מכר. אתם כבר בתמונה.**

## Intended audience reaction

"That's me at 23:40." → "Wait — it just comes in by itself?" → "I want my evening to look like the second one." → click **פתיחת חשבון**.

## Emotional journey

| Beat | Feeling | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| Visual hook | Rhythm, life, a business that is working | Three card/phone taps whose beeps form a melody |
| Tension | Tired, scattered, unsure | Same counter at 23:40, papers, cold screen glare, restless handheld camera |
| Recognition | "I do this" | One honest question on screen: כמה באמת נכנס היום? |
| Discovery | Curiosity, relief beginning | Silence → "אותו יום." + brand → the same tap, now landing by itself in the product |
| Transformation | Order, calm | Same 23:40, same frame — tidy counter, warm lamp, steady camera |
| Confidence | Control, not euphoria | A 2-item attention list, one handled; a small exhale; lid closes; lamp off |
| Action | Clear next step | End card: העסק מכר. אתם כבר בתמונה. + פתיחת חשבון |

## Central creative idea (one sentence)

**We show the owner's same evening twice — once chasing her sales, once when her sales already found their place — and the only thing that changed is Sydney.**

## Before → after

| | Before (Night 1) | After (Night 2) |
|---|---|---|
| Time | 23:40 | 23:40 (identical, on purpose) |
| Counter | Receipts, calculator, notebook of crossed-out sums, phone glowing | Laptop, a steaming mug, notebook closed, phone face-down |
| Light | Cold 6500K screen glare fighting a 2700K lamp; high contrast | Lamp is the key, screen soft and warm-neutral; teal/amber harmony |
| Camera | Subtle restless handheld | Locked off, still |
| Body | Hunched, fingers on brow | Upright, shoulders down, a small exhale |
| Sound | Pen taps, laptop fan, an unresolved drone | Warm resolved chord, then quiet |
| Ending | She's still there | She switches off the lamp and goes home |

## Why this concept fits the business

- It dramatizes the product's actual job: sales arriving by themselves (Grow/Cardcom), one clear figure with its breakdown, and a short list of what needs attention — **all confirmed features**.
- It mirrors the brand's calm, precise, honest voice: no hype, no growth promises, no profit claims.
- The "same evening" device makes the before/after readable with the sound off.
- The sale beeps become the brand's sonic logo: the sound of a sale *is* the sound of Sydney.

## Recommended format

| | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | **45 seconds** master | Enough for a full before/after arc with breathing room; works as the website hero film and as a Meta in-feed video. 15s and 6s cutdowns cover paid placements. |
| Primary format | **16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps** | Website hero/landing page is the primary home. |
| Secondary | **9:16, 1080×1920** adapted (not cropped) for Reels/Stories; **1:1** for feed | Meta placements |
| Platform | sydney-revenue-manager.vercel.app hero + Meta (Instagram/Facebook) | Client decision |

## Visual style

Grounded, intimate realism in one location: a small Tel Aviv pilates studio. Natural textures (whitewashed plaster, pale oak, linen, thermal paper, brass). Photographed like a quiet European short film, not an ad: long-ish lenses, shallow focus, practical light. Product appears as crisp, full-frame UI inserts floating on the brand's deep green — never as fake UI on AI-generated screens.

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep green-black | `#071A15` / `#0B2A22` | Night shadows, UI backgrounds, end card |
| Brand mint | `#41D7B2` | Terminal LED, emphasis word, CTA button, sonic-logo moments |
| Brand teal | `#17745F` / `#47B395` | UI accents, chart line |
| Warm lamp amber | ≈ `#F2B544` (brand accent) | Night practical, skin highlights in Night 2 |
| Plaster white / oak | `#F3F1EC` / `#C9A77C` | Day world |
| Cold screen glare (Night 1 only) | ≈ `#BFD4F2` | The "problem" light — never appears after the turn |

## Lighting approach

Motivated by practicals only: daylight through a steel-framed window by day; a brass desk lamp, a laptop screen and a streetlight at night. The problem is lit with **conflicting color temperatures**; the solution with **harmonized** ones. Same fixtures, different balance.

## Camera language

- Day: gentle slider moves, 50–100 mm macro details, bright and quick.
- Night 1: handheld "breathing" (±1–2% drift), slightly off-level compositions, tighter framings.
- Night 2: locked-off tripod, level horizons, symmetrical re-use of Night 1 framings.
- Master wide (S05/S13/S16) is **one identical composition** used three times.

## Editing rhythm

Fast and musical by day (1.5–3.75 s shots cut on beeps) → slower, uneasy by night (2–2.75 s, cut on small actions) → a hard stop into 1.1 s of silence → a precise, rhythmic product section → long, calm shots at the end → a 6.5 s end card.

## Typography direction

IBM Plex Sans Hebrew (the product's own typeface). One headline style (SemiBold 600), one small time tag (Medium 500, tabular figures, mint), one supporting line (Regular 400). Text enters with a 12 px rise + fade in 400 ms (ease-out-quart — the product's own motion curve) and exits with a 300 ms fade. Right-aligned (RTL), always on clean dark areas.

## Music direction

Warm minimal instrumental — felt piano, soft analog pad, plucked mallet (marimba/kalimba) motif, light brushed pulse, 96 BPM, E major. The three terminal beeps are tuned to **B4 – E5 – G♯5** and become the melodic motif and the final sonic logo. Night 1 is an unresolved drone; the turn is silence; the product section brings the pulse; Night 2 resolves.

## Sound-design direction

Close, tactile, quiet: card taps, the terminal's beep, pencil on paper, thermal paper curling, a laptop fan, a distant scooter, the lamp's switch, the lid's soft close, a door. No whooshes on text. UI sounds are tiny and soft (a single tuned "tick" when a sale lands).

## Final call-to-action strategy

One action, repeated from the website: **פתיחת חשבון** (Open an account) → `sydney-revenue-manager.vercel.app`. The end card restates the brand's promise as a two-line payoff that closes the film's loop ("העסק מכר." opened the film; "אתם כבר בתמונה." completes it).
